## Full description (max 4000 characters)

> **Svartifoss turns your Wear OS watch into a fully customizable remote for
> whatever music is playing on your phone.**
>
> It mirrors the currently playing track — title, artist, album art, playback
> position, queue — from any app that exposes a standard Android media
> session, and lets you control it entirely from your wrist: physical
> buttons, the digital crown, screen taps, and swipe gestures are all
> yours to assign.
>
> No account. No server. No internet connection required for the core
> feature — your phone and watch talk to each other directly.
>
> **On the watch**
> • Now-playing screen with album art and a circular drag-to-seek progress bar
> • Assign any action — play/pause, skip, volume, shuffle, repeat, like,
>   search, playlist shortcuts, opening another app — to physical buttons,
>   screen taps, or swipe gestures
> • A quick-actions panel for shuffle/repeat/like plus a shortcut into the
>   queue
> • Browse the real playback queue, or a local play-history fallback when an
>   app doesn't expose one
> • Voice or keyboard search against the currently playing app's library,
>   with a replayable search history
> • Named playlist shortcuts, with an optional "start shuffled" flag
> • A glanceable Tile with transport controls, a second Tile that previews
>   the next queued track, and a watch-face complication showing the
>   current album art
> • Multiple screen themes and album-art display styles, with independently
>   configurable accent colors
> • Optional rotary-crown seek: turn the crown to scrub the timeline instead
>   of changing volume
>
> **On the phone**
> • One place to configure every button, gesture, and screen on the watch
> • Custom icon and color pickers for personalizing actions
> • Playlist shortcut manager
> • A short in-app guide covering how to use Svartifoss on the watch
>
> **Works with your music app**
> Svartifoss works with any app that publishes a standard Android media
> session. Extra features like like/shuffle/repeat and search rely on
> optional media-session extensions some apps expose — availability varies
> by app.
>
> Svartifoss is free and open source (GPLv3) — a continuation of Music
> Center for Wear by matejdro. Full source code:
> https://github.com/gabrielluizone/Svartifoss
