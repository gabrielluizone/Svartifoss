## Full description (max 4000 characters)

> This is the Wear OS watch companion for **Svartifoss**, the customizable
> phone music remote. It requires the free Svartifoss phone app
> (com.svartifoss.snfell) to work — install that one on your phone first.
>
> Once paired, this app turns your watch into a fully customizable remote
> for whatever music is playing on your phone:
>
> • Now-playing screen with album art and a circular drag-to-seek progress bar
> • Assign play/pause, skip, volume, shuffle, repeat, like, search, playlist
>   shortcuts, or opening another app to physical buttons, screen taps, the
>   digital crown, or swipe gestures
> • A quick-actions panel for shuffle/repeat/like plus a shortcut into the
>   queue
> • Browse the real playback queue, or a local play-history fallback when
>   an app doesn't expose one
> • Voice or keyboard search against the currently playing app's library
> • Named playlist shortcuts, with an optional "start shuffled" flag
> • A glanceable Tile with transport controls, a second Tile that previews
>   the next queued track, and a watch-face complication showing the
>   current album art
> • Multiple screen themes and independently configurable accent colors
> • Optional rotary-crown seek: turn the crown to scrub the timeline
>   instead of changing volume
>
> This is a one-time paid purchase for the watch app — the phone app stays
> free, and its full source code is public (GPLv3):
> https://github.com/gabrielluizone/Svartifoss
>
> Developed and tested primarily against YouTube Music today; broader
> compatibility with other streaming apps is planned in future updates.

(~1350 characters)
