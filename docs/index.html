Svartifoss

The most customizable media controls on Wear OS

Svartifoss reads whatever's playing on your phone — music, podcasts, audiobooks or any other media app — and puts playback, search, queues, lyrics and metadata on your wrist. Every button, gesture, screen and visual layer can be shaped around the way you use your watch.

How it works

Set it up on your phone.
It's on your watch instantly.

Every button, gesture, and screen is yours to assign from the phone app. The watch mirrors it over the local Wearable Data Layer — no account or Svartifoss server. Beyond your two devices, the app talks to GitHub for the optional update check and only when you explicitly open the Community themes gallery, to Firebase for diagnostics and occasional developer announcement notifications, and — only if you opt in — to the streaming service itself to fetch a saved shortcut's cover art. Crashlytics reports, announcement notifications, and shortcut artwork can all be switched off under Data & support → Privacy or Apps.

What you get

Every surface, configurable.

Nothing about Svartifoss is fixed. Pick what each input does, how the screen looks, and what shows up when nothing's bound to a button.

Now-playing screen

Album art, live position, transport controls and a draggable seek surface. Choose from twenty-two in-app faces, including Classic, the original-tribute MatejDro, Expressive, Poster, Studio, Verse, Metadata and the performer-forward Artist — each one rebuilt around the cover that's playing.

Configurable input

Physical buttons, screen quadrants, swipes, the center tap, digital crown, bezel, mini buttons and supported double-pinch gestures can all run different actions while music is playing or stopped.

Quick-actions panel

Double-tap to open a configurable panel with three round slots and one wide row. Pick an action for every slot, or mirror the real actions and icons published by the current player's notification.

Queue & history

Browse a live queue with paging and artwork when the player exposes one, jump to an exact item, or fall back honestly to locally tracked listening history when it doesn't.

Glanceable surfaces

A media Tile with transport and ±10-second seek, a second Tile for saved streaming shortcuts, and an album-art/title complication for the watch face you're already using.

Search & playlists

Search by voice or keyboard, browse the player's library, replay or remove search history, and open saved links to tracks, albums, playlists, artists, shows and mixes.

Full action menu

A full-screen list for anything not bound to a button or gesture — nothing is ever more than one extra tap away.

Works with any player

Any app exposing a standard Android media session can provide basic control. Queues, libraries, search, likes and other extras appear when that player publishes the relevant Android capability.

Every action, on your terms.

Svartifoss is not a fixed three-button remote. It gives the watch a complete action catalogue, then lets you decide which input should run which command.

Content on your wrist

More than a now-playing screen.

Svartifoss stays honest about what the active player makes available: a live queue when there is one, history when there is not, and optional search, lyrics and details only where the phone can retrieve them.

Queue & history

Use the player's queue when it exists.

Browse the current playback queue, request more items and jump to a track. If the active player does not publish a queue, Svartifoss shows its locally tracked listening history instead.

Search, library & lists

Ask the player for what it knows.

Search by voice or keyboard, replay recent searches, browse a MediaBrowser library when the app offers one, and keep saved playlist shortcuts reachable from the watch.

Lyrics & Metadata

Optional, on-demand detail.

Lyrics are requested only when a lyrics surface or Verse needs them. Metadata is built from player tags, local-file details where permitted, and optional MusicBrainz enrichment — it never invents information the source does not provide.

Not a coat of paint — every surface, really tunable.

Save a look as a named theme and switch between them in one tap. Each profile can combine a base face, typography, colors, artwork, background layers, progress, overlays, panels, mini buttons and the always-on display. Every gallery miniature is rendered on your phone from the theme data against a built-in sample track.

Per-face editing

One watch, different moods.

Store appearance independently for each face instead of forcing one global look. Change title, artist, clock, lyrics and track-time typography; choose album treatments, color harmony, progress style, panel layout and custom button shapes.

Always-on display

Designed for the low-power screen too.

Choose an always-on presentation, artwork treatment, dim level, visibility and typography separately from the interactive player. The phone preview lets you check the ambient result before it reaches the watch.

Readable by design

Make text hold its ground.

Use album-aware or custom colors, adjustable opacity, shadow, outline and backdrop treatments for the title and artist. The same visual system also covers volume, seek, panels and queue rows.

On the phone

Preview before you send.

The Watch tab mirrors the current track or a built-in sample and updates as you edit. Apply, duplicate, rename or delete profiles without changing the others.

Compatibility & privacy

Built around Android's media standards.

Svartifoss does not replace your music app, stream audio or ask for a Svartifoss cloud account. It connects the phone and watch locally, then uses the media contracts each installed player makes available.

Local first

Your controls do not pass through a Svartifoss server.

The phone and watch talk over the local Wearable Data Layer. Ordinary control works without creating an account or giving a third party your listening activity.

The name

Black Falls

Svartifoss means "Black Falls" in Icelandic — named after the waterfall in Vatnajökull National Park, where dark basalt columns frame a narrow cascade. Like water over stone, music flows through the app.

Install

Sideload it. Takes a minute.

Svartifoss isn't on the Play Store — install the two APKs directly from GitHub Releases onto your phone and watch. The phone needs Android notification access to discover the active media session; the watch app is installed separately. After the first setup, the phone can notify you about releases and send watch updates over Bluetooth.

Install on your phone

Download the phone APK below and open it. Android may ask you to allow installs from your browser first.

Install on your watch

Download the watch APK and sideload it to your Wear OS watch — easiest with Wear Installer .

Open both apps

They find each other automatically over the Wearable Data Layer — no account, no pairing code, nothing else to configure.
